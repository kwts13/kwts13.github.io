User stories are cheap to write and expensive to get wrong. Before I write one, I try to answer three questions.

## 1. Who is this actually for?

"As a user" isn't an answer. If I can't name a real person or role, the story isn't ready.

## 2. What do they do next?

Most stories describe the moment of the feature and stop. What happens in the minute afterwards usually shows whether it was the right thing to build.

## 3. How will we know it worked?

If the answer is "when it's released", that's a delivery milestone, not an outcome. I want something that someone could check a month later.

## Why bother

None of these take long. All of them are easier to answer before the work starts than during a retrospective.
