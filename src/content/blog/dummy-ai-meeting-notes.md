I wanted to find out whether AI note-taking is genuinely useful or just a new way to feel organised. So for a month, I used it for every meeting I could.

## What worked

Summaries were quick and mostly accurate. Action items were the best part: having a draft list the moment a meeting ended meant I actually sent follow-ups.

## What didn't

It was confidently wrong about names, dates and, occasionally, who agreed to what. A decision that was clearly "let's discuss next week" turned into "approved" in one summary. That's the kind of mistake that causes real problems, because it reads so cleanly.

## How I use it now

- Treat the output as a draft, never as a record.
- Check every decision and every date before it goes anywhere.
- Keep my own two-line summary of what was actually decided.

It saves time. It doesn't remove the need to pay attention.
