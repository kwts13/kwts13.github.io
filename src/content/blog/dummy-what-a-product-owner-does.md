If you only read job ads, a Product Owner sets the vision, owns the roadmap and delights customers. In practice, a good chunk of the day is smaller and less glamorous.

## A rough breakdown

- **Clarifying.** Answering the same question for the third team in a slightly different way.
- **Prioritising.** Deciding what doesn't happen this sprint, and telling someone.
- **Unblocking.** Finding the person who can approve the thing and making it easy for them to say yes.
- **Checking.** Looking at what shipped and asking whether it did what we said it would.

## The skill nobody lists

The underrated skill is saying "not yet" without losing the room. Most ideas are good ideas. The job is working out the order, and explaining the order honestly.

I came to product from business analysis, and the overlap is bigger than the titles suggest. The main difference is that you now have to live with your own answers.
