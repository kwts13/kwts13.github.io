Every tabletop group has a spreadsheet that started as a good idea. Ours tracks characters, loot and who owes the party money. It has grown, as these things do.

## The idea

A small app to replace it: a character list, a shared inventory and a session log, with as little setup as possible. Nothing that needs an account.

## What I got wrong

I started by building features. I should have started by watching how people actually use the spreadsheet during a session. It turns out they mostly want to look something up in under five seconds, and everything else is secondary.

## What I'd tell myself

- Do the boring research first. Watch real use before writing code.
- Build the one thing people reach for most, and ship that.
- A side project that gets used beats one that gets finished.
